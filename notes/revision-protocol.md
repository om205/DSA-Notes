# Weekly Revision Session — Protocol

- **Cadence:** every **Sunday**. Started 2026-08-16.
- **Budget:** 45–60 minutes. Do not let it sprawl.
- **Rule:** if a week passes without one, the mentor reminds me.

> **Why this exists:** notes were being generated prolifically and never retrieved. That is exactly why the sliding-window skeleton was written down and *still* not reached for — twice in a row. **Notes you don't retrieve are notes you didn't write.**

---

## The one principle

**Retrieval, not re-reading.** Re-reading feels productive and builds almost nothing. Answer from memory *first*, then open the file to check. The struggle to recall IS the learning.
